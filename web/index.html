How it works

  1. The real game. Chromium's own T-Rex runner source, pinned and unedited. Only its clock and dice are pinned, so any run replays exactly on any browser.
  2. A tiny brain. The dino sees 20 numbers (distance and time to the next obstacles, their shape, its own height, speed) and holds JUMP or DUCK. No ML framework: NEAT in a few hundred lines of TypeScript.
  3. Crash → lesson. Every crash is saved as a snapshot 3 s before impact. The curriculum replays those moments until the brain clears them.
  4. Proof, not a claim. A champion is promoted only after 500 fresh tracks × 30 minutes at top speed with zero crashes, plus every past crash.
